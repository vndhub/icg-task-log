ICG Weekly Task Log

Log your tasks as you go during the week. When done, hit Send to copy a summary to your clipboard, then paste it to Quynh via Zalo, email, or Microsoft Teams.

Codebook
FC (Functional Contribution)
— Your regular, recurring duties from your job description. Examples: client meetings, daily follow-ups, preparing reports, responding to client requests.
OC (Organizational Contribution)
— Cross-functional or improvement initiatives beyond your daily job. Examples: helping build a new process, contributing to a company-wide project, proposing a new idea.
Status options:
Planned = scheduled but not started. In progress = actively working on it. Done = completed. Blocked = stuck, waiting on someone or something else.

Auto-saved — your data stays on this device even if you close the browser.
